Our Story

Bankai Retro started with one closet full of old football shirts and a simple observation: nobody was making it easy for South African fans to find authentic, vintage-style kits. What began as a side hustle selling shirts at local matches and markets has grown into a small but dedicated business built entirely on Instagram and word of mouth.

We're run by football fans, for football fans. Every kit in our collection is chosen because it means something: a title-winning season, a legendary player, a club's golden era. See what we have right now in our retro football kit collection.

Our Mission

To make football heritage wearable again, by sourcing authentic retro kits and making them accessible to a new generation of fans who value nostalgia and streetwear culture over mass-produced replicas.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's go-to online destination for retro and vintage football kits, trusted by fans from Johannesburg to anywhere the game is loved.

Who We Serve

Football fans aged 18–35 who care about club heritage, nostalgia and streetwear style. These are supporters who would rather wear a shirt with a story than one fresh off a factory shelf. Looking for a particular shirt? Send us an enquiry or come and meet us at the market.
